Cookie Policy

1. About This Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Kwikshift Movers uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. In this policy, “Kwikshift Movers”, “we”, “us”, and “our” refer to the moving and relocation services business operating this website.

This policy applies to the websites and web pages we operate for our services, including kwikshiftmovers.co.ke, www.kwikshiftmovers.co.ke, and any related landing pages or supported subdomains we manage for customer enquiries, quotations, and service communication.

2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files created when you browse a website. They are sent to your computer or mobile device and stored by your web browser. Cookies perform a range of functions, such as helping a website remember your preferences, keeping parts of the website secure, and understanding how visitors use the site.

Clear gifs, pixel trackers, web beacons, and similar technologies work in a comparable way. They may be embedded within pages or content to help measure activity on the site. In this Cookie Policy, references to cookies also include these related technologies where applicable.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Kwikshift Movers may use the following categories of cookies on supported pages of our website.

3.1 Necessary Cookies

Necessary cookies help make the website usable by enabling core functions such as page navigation, basic security, consent management, and access to features required for the website to operate properly.

3.2 Preferences

Preference cookies store information that changes how the website behaves or appears, such as remembering useful settings or maintaining your selected experience where relevant.

3.3 Statistics and Performance

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, including which pages are viewed more often, how users move through the site, and where we can improve performance, clarity, or usability.

3.4 Marketing

Marketing cookies may be used to measure campaign performance, understand how visitors arrive at our website, and support more relevant promotional activity where optional marketing tools are enabled.

  • We may use cookies to understand how visitors navigate to and use our website so we can improve our marketing and communication performance.
  • Third-party marketing or analytics partners may also set cookies where their tools are enabled and you have consented to the relevant cookie category.

4. Information Cookies Collect and Share

The information collected through cookies and similar technologies may include:

  • The way you use our website, including the pages you visit, buttons you click, files you view, and how long you spend on certain sections.
  • Search terms, site interactions, and navigation paths used while browsing.
  • Information about the device, operating system, and web browser used to access the site.
  • Session identifiers, browser version details, and technical performance data.
  • Language or display preferences where supported.
  • Approximate location or region information derived from browser or network signals.
  • Information entered into forms before submission where this is required for functionality, continuity, or security.

5. How to Manage Your Preferences and Settings

5.1 Cookie Banner and Preference Controls

On supported pages of our website, you can manage optional cookies through our cookie consent and preferences controls. When available, the cookie banner may appear automatically on your first visit, and you can review categories such as Preferences, Statistics and Performance, and Marketing before saving your choices.

If you change your mind later, you can reopen the cookie preferences controls where that feature is available and update your settings. Disabling optional categories will stop new data from being collected through those categories from that point forward, although previously collected information may still remain subject to earlier consent and lawful processing.

5.2 Cross-Domain Consent Sharing

Where technically supported, your consent preferences may be shared across related Kwikshift Movers domains to simplify cookie management.

  • The domains currently intended to share consent are kwikshiftmovers.co.ke and www.kwikshiftmovers.co.ke.
  • Your browser settings and technical limitations may affect whether consent choices carry across all related pages consistently.
  • If you visit another related page or domain and your consent does not carry over, you may be asked to confirm your preferences again.

5.3 Browser-Specific Settings

Your cookie choices are generally stored per browser and device. This means preferences set in one browser may not automatically apply in another browser or on a different device.

You can also manage cookies directly through your browser settings. Please note that blocking some categories may affect site quality, convenience, or functionality. Ad blockers or similar tools may also interfere with cookie banners and preference controls.

6. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Kwikshift Movers does not sell your personal information. However, some optional marketing or analytics activity may be treated in certain jurisdictions as a form of sharing or disclosure of personal information for advertising or measurement purposes.

If you do not want optional marketing-related cookies to be used, you should disable the Marketing category through our cookie preferences controls where available, or adjust your browser settings to limit such cookies. You may also contact us by email if you have questions about how these settings apply to your browsing experience.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, site features, or our data practices. When we make important changes, we may update the content of this page and revise the “Last updated” date shown above.

We encourage you to review this Cookie Policy periodically. Changes become effective when they are posted on this page unless a different timing is required by law.

8. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or need help understanding your cookie choices, please contact us by email at info@kwikshiftmovers.co.ke.
